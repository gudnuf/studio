# Brand: Jefe

Source: usejefe.com landing copy (2026-11-07). This is a stand-in until the
intellectual-signature interview produces a real brand document.

## What it is
Jefe: "Say it out loud. Get the paperwork." You walk a job and talk it through,
the way you'd tell a helper, and Jefe has the estimate written before you're back
in the truck. iPhone only. Your first five walks are free with no card, then it's
$39/mo or $299.99/yr.

## Who it's for
Landscapers, property managers and home inspectors. People who work in the field
and hate typing up paperwork at night.

## What's true and can be claimed
- **Your audio never leaves your phone.** The listening happens on the device. Only
  the typed-out words go to the AI provider, and none of it is used for training.
- **It's a first draft, not a verdict.** Every line can be edited. Jefe flags what it
  didn't hear instead of guessing, so you never send a number you didn't choose.
- **Three steps:** walk and talk, fix anything, send it. Output can be an estimate,
  invoice, work order or report, with your letterhead.
- **No account and no sign-up.** You can be walking a job in under a minute.
- **Built by a one-person shop in California.** "If you email, you get me."

## Voice
Plain, physical, field-first: trucks, driveways, mulch, sprinkler heads. Short
sentences. Never hype, never "revolutionize," never AI buzzwords. It sounds like
one tradesperson talking to another. Founder-led and honest about limits
("iPhone only for now").

## Content pillars (draft)
1. **The paperwork tax:** the hours after the job spent typing estimates.
2. **Walk it, say it, send it:** real walks with real output, shown and not told.
3. **Your words stay yours:** on-device audio, and the operator stays in control.
4. **Building it:** a small shop making a tool for the field, out loud.

## Never claim
- Don't make up customer counts, testimonials, revenue or time-saved numbers.
- No Android version. Nothing that isn't in the app today.
